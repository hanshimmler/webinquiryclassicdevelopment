If AKos already received inquiries or orders for a given compound in the past we have answers from all potential suppliers of the compound with information about if the compound can be delivered, together with information about availability of the compound (i.e. does the supplier has the compound on stock or if the compound can be resynthesized). Every supplier we did contact that confirms that the compound can be delivered is treated as 'Known supplier'. Prices for known suppliers (if available) are exclusive additional costs such as AKos commission and/or other additional costs.
