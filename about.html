About us

For more than 25 years AKOS has supported various customers with tailored chemical solutions. With a long history rooted in excellence, we specialize in sourcing high-quality chemicals and facilitating custom synthesis projects for a diverse range of clients.

Our mission is to be the leading provider of chemical solutions, connecting clients with top-quality products and custom synthesis services. We strive to foster innovation, collaboration, and excellence in everything we do, empowering our clients to accelerate procurement process and achieve their goals and advance science and technology worldwide.

Our unique constantly updated database (>40 M structures) of molecules for different purposes, extensive industry knowledge and a vast network of suppliers allow us to deliver tailored solutions that meet your unique requirements. From Building Blocks, Screening compounds to Bioactive Probes and Drug Conjugates, we are committed to providing reliable, cost-effective, and timely services.

Leveraging our network of trusted suppliers and contractors, we can easily source the ideal partner for custom synthesis needs ensuring rapid and cost-effective solutions for our customers.
