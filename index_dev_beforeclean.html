Do you want to create a quick request?
AKos quick request
Let us know what compound you would like to inquiry. You can also inquire a list of compounds. We will come back to you with quotation and availability details.
How to enter your query?
You can use the following information that describe your molecule : AKos number, CAS, Molname, Molformula, InChI, Smiles, PubChem CID.
f none of the above matches your needs, feel free to express your molecule details using free text.
If possible we appreciate you also let us know the amount you would like to inquire for a given compound.
NEW: AKos inquiry Android phone and tablet app
The app allows you to perform the identical tasks as our webpage with some extensions such as reusable search history, chemical intelligent auto-completion (PubChem), possibility to save contact information. The app supports so-called mobile deep linking for PubChem/ChemSpider and ChemExper AKos links. That means that clicking an AKos compound vendor link on e.g. PubChem/ChemSpider/ChemExper web pages opens the app instead of using a browser to open a web page.
