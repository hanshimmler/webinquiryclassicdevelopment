When to use an assisted request?
Our standard inquiry page is designed for inquiring a single or very few compounds. In all other cases we recommend to use the assisted request. Use cases of an assisted request:
  • You prefer that the AKos team supports you resolving your inquiry information.
  • You want to inquiry many compounds.
  • You do not get any any hits for your query.
  • You want to submit a complex inquiry.
  • You are looking for custom synthesis.
If none of the above matches your needs, feel free to express your molecule details using free text.

How does an assisted request work?
We provide a form where you can enter your inquiry using free text. Typical examples of information you can provide are:
  • CAS Number
  • Mol Name or Synonym Name
  • Mol Formula
  • Smiles or InChI Name
  • PubChem/ChemSpider ID
The AKos team takes over the task of finding the corresponding chemical compounds for the inquiry information you provided and get back to you with a list of possible suppliers. In case of uncertainties or problems the AKos team work together with you to resolve any issues.
