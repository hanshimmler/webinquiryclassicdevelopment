AKos compounds requests/orders explained
Do you want to create an assisted request?
Select the AKos number(s) you would like to add to the assisted request.
Otherwise click the OK button without any selection.
AKos assisted request
Let us know what compound you would like to inquiry. You can also inquire a list of compounds. We will come back to you with quotation and availability details.
How to enter your query?
You can use the following information that describe your molecule : AKos Number, CAS Number, Mol Name, Mol Formula, InChI, Smiles, PubChem/ChemSpider ID.
If none of the above matches your needs, feel free to express your molecule details using free text.
If possible we appreciate you also let us know the amount you would like to inquire for a given compound.
How to request/order compounds with AKos
Offer
  • We send you an offer that shows all possible suppliers of the compound
Verification
  • If you are satisfied with an offer and order one or more substances, we will first send you the corresponding structures.
Delivery notification
  • When one or more substances are ready for delivery, you will receive written notification from us.
Import documents
  • If you require specific documents for import, we will send them to you as soon as possible.
